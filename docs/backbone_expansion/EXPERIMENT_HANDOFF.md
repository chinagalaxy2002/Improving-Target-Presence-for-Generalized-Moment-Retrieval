## Adaptation and verification

All three are adapted backbone-GMR models, not upstream GMR models. Each adds the same two-layer MLP over max-pooled final decoder queries, with the actual decoder dimension; GEQ reads declared positive-branch encoder features. BM uses upstream slowfast/clip order, probability guidance and contrastive losses, plus cross-video synthetic negative text on Train only; it does not reinterpret synthetic pairs as real null labels. Its matching/classification trains real nulls as background. Same-video contrastive pairs are excluded. The chosen QV-style upstream profile does not enable the optional Charades temporal-shift augmentation. Saliency pairs are derived from the union of GT windows, and omitted when no foreground/background pair exists.

BAM uses 768-dimensional final decoder features, retains quality and boundary losses, and ranks candidates by the upstream quality score itself. Its null targets have zero quality, boundary targets use each real video length, and same-video rotated synthetic pairs are excluded. UVCOM reads `CIM.GKA_Module` after the global-token offset; its similarity and saliency losses use real positive/null labels and all windows, exclude same-video synthetic negatives, and preserve the native negative-selection epoch with the GMR best epoch as the GEQ offset. GEQ captures the first positive encoder pass. All three supervise their GMR head throughout GEQ Full.

Bounded checks are stored in `runs/verification/expansion_bm_final`, `expansion_bam_final`, and `expansion_uvcom_01`. They are functional tests, not convergence results. Prior runs verified mixed multi-window/null and all-null gradients, GEQ/native gradients, interruption/resumption against continuous model tensors, GEQ joint updates, strict loading, and both test entry points. Additional full untrained GEQ inference checked exact qid coverage and 94/104-token Full videos; reports are under the corresponding verification directories (BM coverage in `expansion_bm_04/full_coverage.json`, BAM in `expansion_bam_01/full_coverage.json`, UVCOM in `expansion_uvcom_01/full_coverage.json`). Full-foreground finite loss/gradient and UVCOM epoch 70 were checked. Synthetic tests do not establish quality or convergence.

All five pre-existing backbone regression runs passed under `runs/verification/expansion_regression_*`. The final BM and BAM verifiers passed on the frozen source; BM also passed exact resume with two data-loader workers. Confirm the source identity before any formal dispatch. Tests `tests/test_expansion.py` cover BAM quality ordering and same-video synthetic-negative masking.

A 12-task synthetic DAG test passed, including the final report dispatch. The reporting script also passed an end-to-end test against a bounded BM run. All gates described above passed. The queue was launched as user systemd service `soccergmr-backbone-expansion-20261102.service`, after a `nohup` attempt failed to survive. Its root is `runs/backbone_expansion/formal`; `queue_state.json` records live PID, GPU, logs and dependencies. Explicit machine-specific configs use the audited paths and Full-Decodable label. ## Live training monitor

`runs/backbone_expansion/formal/monitor/training_val_panel.png` (and SVG) is refreshed once per minute by the user timer `soccergmr-expansion-monitor.timer`. It plots Standard Val mAP for each stage; stars mark the stage-specific Val selection and oracle numbers use the selected fixed candidates. GEQ selects by mean G-mIoU@1, so its selected mAP need not be maximal. Missing data appear as waiting. The timer reads only Standard Val predictions and histories and never influences training or Test selection. Check with `systemctl --user status soccergmr-expansion-monitor.timer` and `journalctl --user -u soccergmr-expansion-monitor.service`. This is a plot updater, not an agent wake-up or recovery service.

## Incremental Test figures

Each completed GMR or GEQ stage is followed by its own Standard and Full-Decodable Test task in the queue. User timer `soccergmr-incremental-test-monitor.timer` independently regenerates first- and second-batch `*_batch_{standard,full}_live.{png,svg}` in the monitor directory once a minute. It shows only finalized Val-selected checkpoint tests with matching completion hashes, plots 101 threshold points and each available method's mAP, and adds oracle and GEQ-minus-GMR deltas once available. Missing stages remain blank. The initial eight completed first-batch Test splits matched the official 0.4/0.5/0.6 metrics. These plots are descriptive only and do not select training parameters.
